Storm damage in West Yorkshire is a matter of when, not if. Ridge tiles come off, slates lift, lead gets peeled back and a chimney that was already weak decides it has had enough. We offer 24/7 emergency call-outs for storm damage, we make the roof safe, and we give you what you need to make an insurance claim. We cover insurance work.

## What happens when you call

1. **We make it safe.** Temporary sheeting, slates refixed or removed before they fall, loose lead secured. The aim on the first visit is to stop water coming in and stop anything falling.
2. **We document it.** Photos from the roof, a written description of the damage, and a quote for the permanent repair, in a format insurers are used to.
3. **You claim.** You send the report and quote to your insurer. If they want to talk to the roofer, we are happy to deal with the loss adjuster on the roofing side.
4. **We repair.** Once the claim is agreed, we carry out the full repair or, where the damage has finished off an old roof, the [re-roof](/services/re-roofing/).

## Being straight about wear and tear

Insurers pay for storm damage, not for roofs that were already failing. If a storm has exposed a roof that was on its last legs, we will say so in the report. It is better to know where you stand than to have a claim rejected after weeks of waiting.

## Not an insurance claim?

Plenty of storm damage is below the excess or not worth claiming for. We repair those jobs on a normal quote, and we will tell you if that is the sensible route.
