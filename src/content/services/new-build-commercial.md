New-build roofing is about getting it right first time, to drawings, on programme, with no snagging list. We roof new houses, extensions, small developments and commercial buildings across West Yorkshire, in natural slate, slate-effect and concrete tile, with single-ply for flat areas, and every roof carries our 25-year workmanship guarantee.

## What we bring to a new build

- **One roofer for the whole roof.** Slate or tile, battens and membrane, leadwork, dry ridge and verge, flat roof areas, roof windows and lanterns.
- **Pricing from drawings**, with the materials named and anything that will not work on site flagged early.
- **Programme discipline.** We turn up when the roof is ready and we finish before the follow-on trades need the building dry.
- **Natural slate expertise.** Stone-built new houses in Yorkshire look right under slate, and slate laid well is what the drone photos on this site show.
- **Site-ready.** Working to the principal contractor's rules, with £10 million public liability cover.

## Who we work with

Builders and small developers, self-builders managing their own project, and architects who want a roofer who will build the detail they have drawn. If you are a homeowner building an extension, your builder can bring us in or you can book us directly.

## Commercial roofing

For commercial and larger buildings we fit single-ply membrane flat roofs and tile or slate pitched roofs, and we take on re-roofing of existing commercial property where access and programme can be managed. Tell us what the building is and we will say whether it is a job for us.
