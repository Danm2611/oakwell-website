  - q: Should I replace fascias when I re-roof?
    a: It is the cheapest time to do it. The scaffold is up, the bottom course of tiles is off, and the new fascia goes in before the roof is finished. If your fascias are sound we leave them; if they are soft or peeling, we will tell you.
  - q: Do you cap over the old timber?
    a: No. Capping traps damp behind the plastic and the timber keeps rotting. We take the old boards off, check the rafter feet, and fix new boards to sound timber.
  - q: My gutters overflow in heavy rain. Can you fix that?
    a: Usually, yes. Overflowing is nearly always a fall or a joint problem rather than a capacity problem. We re-hang the gutter to a proper fall, replace failed joints and clean the downpipes. Where a roof genuinely needs more capacity we fit deeper profile gutter.

Fascias, soffits and gutters are the edge of the roof, and the edge is where the water ends up. Rotten fascia boards let the gutter sag, a sagging gutter overflows down the wall, and before long you have damp in the bedroom corner. We replace timber fascias and soffits with uPVC, ventilate the roof space properly and hang the gutters so they actually drain.

## What we fit

- **uPVC fascias** in white, black, anthracite and woodgrain finishes, fixed to sound timber.
- **Ventilated soffits** so the loft can breathe, which matters more than ever on well-insulated houses.
- **Guttering** in half-round, square or deep-flow profile, re-hung to a proper fall with new brackets.
- **Downpipes** cleared, replaced and connected to the drain properly.
- **Dry verge** to gable ends, replacing cracked mortar for good.
- **Bargeboards** and box ends to match.

## Done with the roof

Most of our fascia and gutter work is done as part of a [re-roof](/services/re-roofing/), when the bottom course is off and the scaffold is up. It adds relatively little to the job and it means the whole edge of the roof is new at once. We also do fascias and gutters as a stand-alone job from ladders or a tower where the roof itself is fine.

## Gutter cleaning and repairs

If the gutters are sound but blocked, leaking at a joint or pulling away from the wall, we repair rather than replace. We will tell you which it is.
